# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
**Standard Form for Client Software Engagements**  
**Framework:** Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023  
**Version:** 2026.1  

---

**This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA")** is entered into by and between:

1. **The Client** (acting as **"Data Controller"** or **"Controller"**); and
2. **Rayida Tech** (acting as **"Data Processor"** or **"Processor"**), having its primary digital operations at [https://rayidatech.name.ng](https://rayidatech.name.ng), email: [info@rayidatech.name.ng](mailto:info@rayidatech.name.ng).

---

## 1. Scope & Application
This DPA supplements the Principal Agreement between the parties whenever Rayida Tech processes personal data on behalf of the Controller during the provision of custom software development, cloud infrastructure integration, AI systems, or managed application services under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

## 2. Processor Obligations
1. **Documented Instructions:** Process personal data strictly in accordance with the Controller's written instructions.
2. **Confidentiality:** Ensure all staff and contractors authorized to access personal data are bound by formal confidentiality obligations.
3. **Security Standards:** Maintain industry-standard technical measures (TLS 1.3, AES-256 encryption, access controls) under Section 39 of the NDPA 2023.
4. **Sub-processors:** Maintain vetted infrastructure partners (Vercel, Supabase, AWS, Resend) under equivalent written data protection standards.
5. **Cross-Border Safeguards:** Conduct any international data transfers in full compliance with Part VIII of the NDPA 2023.
6. **Breach Notification:** Notify the Controller **within 48 hours** of becoming aware of any confirmed personal data breach affecting Controller data, to enable the Controller to satisfy the statutory 72-hour NDPC notification requirement.
7. **Data Return & Deletion:** At the Controller's election upon contract conclusion, securely delete or return all personal data copies, unless retention is required by Nigerian law.
